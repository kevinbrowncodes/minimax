# ComfyUI PR #15375 — Support per-token video and audio latent noise masks on MiniMax-H3

https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/pull/15375 · merged 2026-08-18T03:18:18Z

Video masks snap to the 2x2 latent patch grid, audio masks to whole latent frames, both rounded to binary.

Enables binary masking on video and audio latents. Internally clamps the masks by thresholding around alpha of 0.5 to set to 0 or 1 values and broadcasts to the 2x2 latent patches, so the mask is slightly larger and blockier than a typical latent mask, as required by the row size.
