As the owner, I want the Assets page — its title and tabs, the filter chips and the search field, the video tiles with the reference's ⋯ menu, the empty states and the preview dialog — to look and move like agent.minimax.io's in both themes and at both widths, so that the gallery of finished videos reads as the reference.

## Current state

STORY_015's Assets page has the title, three tabs (the first real, the others inert), the eight chips, the search field, poster tiles with a ▶ Preview overlay and a ⋯ menu (Open task / Extend / Download / Delete from history), a preview `<dialog>` with ⤓ ↗ × in its head, and an empty state ("No assets yet" for All, "No `<kind>` yet" for a chip). Against the captures: the reference's tabs read **From agent / From you / Star** and all three work (From you and Star show the empty state); its chips are 32 px tall with an 8 px gap and **every** chip other than the one with content shows the shared "No assets yet" state; its tile is 252 × 182 with a 40 px name row (a camera icon, the name, a ⋯ that is always there at 1440); its ⋯ menu reads **Locate in task / Send to new task / Star / Delete** (red); at 390 it has no page title, tabs or search field — the top bar carries "Assets" with a search and a filter icon, the chips scroll (labelled **Website** and **Document** there), the tiles are two 168 × 95 posters with the name under them and no ⋯, and the preview is a full-screen sheet whose head is **×**, the bold name and **⋯**.

## Departures from the reference

- **Send to new task** opens the task extending the video (STORY_016): the reference attaches the asset to a fresh task, which for a video means continuing it; that is the one "new task from this asset" MiniMax Local has.
- **Download** stays reachable — in the preview's ⋯ menu — because the reference's entries were never clicked (recon was read-only) and a video the owner cannot save is useless here.
- The poster shows the job's first frame (the reference's tile shows a grey area with a camera glyph — its videos have no poster on this page).
- Tabs and chips keep our entries and labels at 1440 (Websites / Documents); the 390 labels follow the 390 capture (Website / Document).
- The 1440 preview dialog's head is the 390 sheet's, the only head captured.
